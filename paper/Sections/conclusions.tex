% Conclusions
Ultimately, value nodes have shown their versatility in performing complex tasks.
There are however some massive drawbacks as it is much more computationally 
heavy to do individual calculations than with matrix operations. In the future 
with a matrix implementation, it may be better to utilize the Muon optimizer
alongside data standardization with synthetic data. With better generalizations with the dataset, 
we can push this minilistic, bare bones implementation to the limit. In whole, regardless of potential tweaks, 
this model's ability is not limited by its simplicity.
